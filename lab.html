        Instructions

        Workspaces

        • Theory: concepts, equations, and the model diagram.
        • Explore: work through the stages. The buttons along the bottom of the canvas control the displayed layers.
        • Analyze: readings, the field-synthesis guide, geometry, and linked curves.
        • Record: the study log, where you can record readings and export them as a CSV file.

        Controls

        • Drag to rotate, Shift-drag or right-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. You can drag the probe and the charges directly.
        • Shortcuts: 1–4 switch workspaces, [ ] switch stages, ← → switch synthesis steps, P presents, and Esc exits.

        Color

        • Orange "+" is positive, blue "−" is negative, and gray "0" is the zero-potential level.
